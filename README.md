# Lumacode Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 August 2026**

Lumacode ("Lumacode", "we", "us", or "our") operates [lumacode.io](https://lumacode.io). This policy explains how we handle personal information submitted through our website.

## Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, company, and the contents of your message. We collect this information only so that we can respond to your enquiry and communicate with you about it.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data through our contact process. Please do not send sensitive information through the website.

## How we use information

We use contact information to:

- respond to enquiries;
- communicate with you about your request; and
- keep limited records where reasonably necessary to manage our communications and protect our legitimate interests.

We do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising.

## Sharing and service providers

We may use carefully selected providers that help us operate the website or receive and respond to enquiries. They may process information only as needed to provide those services and under appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We do not otherwise share contact information unless required by law or necessary to protect our rights.

## Retention

We keep contact information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the enquiry, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may ask us to delete it, subject to information we must retain by law.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, restriction or objection to processing, and receipt of a copy of your information. You can also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent and complain to your local data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, contact us at [info@lumacode.io](mailto:info@lumacode.io). We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Our website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
